The Philosophy

Every dish at Provision is built around real macros — protein, carbohydrates, fats — in ratios that make sense for people who care about what they put in their bodies. No gimmicks, no diet culture. Well-crafted food with honest numbers on the side.

Field Guide

Fast Food Hacks

Know what to order before you walk through the door. The best macro plays at the chains you actually eat at.

Chick-fil-A
The Move
Order the Grilled Cool Wrap without even looking at the rest of the menu. 42g protein for 350 calories is better than most things you could make at home. Need more? Add an 8-count grilled nuggets on the side and you're at 67g protein for 490 cal total.
Grilled Cool Wrap
Grilled chicken, lettuce, cheese in a flaxseed flatbread. The best macro item at any fast food chain, period. Ask for the dressing on the side.
~42g 350 cal
Grilled Nuggets — 8 Count
25g protein for 140 calories. The most efficient item on the menu by a wide margin. Pair with a fruit cup if you need volume.
~25g 140 cal
Market Salad (Grilled Chicken)
Grilled chicken, apple, berries, blue cheese crumbles, granola. Use the light balsamic — skip the full dressing packet, it doubles the calories.
~30g ~340 cal
Taco Bell
The Move
Cantina Chicken Bowl, Fresco style. "Fresco" swaps out cheese and sour cream for pico de gallo — saves ~100 cal with zero protein loss. Avoid the creamy jalapeño sauce, the beef options, and the nachos fries that will show up on every screen you walk past.
Cantina Chicken Bowl
Rice, black beans, grilled chicken, pico, avocado ranch. The most complete macro meal on the Taco Bell menu. Get it Fresco to cut calories.
~32g ~490 cal
Chicken Power Bowl (Fresco)
Grilled chicken, rice, black beans, guac, reduced-cal with the Fresco swap. Solid backup when the Cantina Bowl is sold out.
~26g ~370 cal
Chicken Soft Tacos × 3 (Fresco)
Three is the number. Fresco cuts the cheese and sour cream. Each runs about 160 cal. Easy to eat, easy on the macros.
~27g ~480 cal
Wendy's
The Move
Grilled Chicken Sandwich + Small Chili instead of fries. That's ~50g protein for ~530 cal total and the chili genuinely earns its spot — it's not an afterthought. Ask for mayo on the side or skip it entirely.
Grilled Chicken Sandwich
Full chicken breast, good bun, legitimately good. No mayo or on the side. Skip the combo upgrade — the fries aren't worth it here.
~34g ~370 cal
Small Chili
The most underrated side in fast food. Beans, ground beef, tomatoes, spices. 16g protein for 160 cal. Pair it with anything — it always works.
~16g ~160 cal
Grilled Chicken Wrap
Light option when you want something smaller. Ranch on the side. Pairs well with the chili or a side salad to round out the meal.
~24g ~270 cal
Taco Time
The Move
Three Chicken Soft Tacos is your order. Skip the Mexi Fries unless you've saved the budget — they're great but burn 400+ cal on a side. Ask for light cheese and no sour cream to tighten it up without losing any of the experience.
Chicken Soft Tacos × 3
Their best high-protein order. Light cheese, no sour cream. Salsa keeps it honest and still tastes exactly right. This is the play.
~27g ~450 cal
Chicken Burrito
Good when you want volume and something more filling. Skip the sour cream inside, use salsa instead. Stays manageable.
~28g ~520 cal
Crispy Chicken Tacos × 2
Their classic. Slightly higher cal than soft but worth it occasionally. Don't add extra sauce — the taco is already seasoned well.
~22g ~420 cal
Chipotle
The Move
Double chicken bowl. Brown rice, black beans, fajita veggies, fresh salsa, guac. The double protein is ~$3 more and takes you from 21g to 42g — that's the only math that matters. Skip the sour cream and use salsa as your sauce.
Double Chicken Burrito Bowl
Brown rice, black beans, fajita veggies, fresh salsa, guac. This is the Provision house order, made off-site. No-brainer every time.
~42g ~683 cal
Single Chicken Bowl (Cauliflower Rice)
Swap brown rice for cauli rice — cuts ~170 cal, same protein. Black beans and fajita veggies keep the volume up. Good on lighter days.
~32g ~380 cal
Double Steak Bowl
Same build as the chicken bowl. Steak runs slightly higher fat but the protein numbers hold. Change of pace without changing the strategy.
~40g ~700 cal
Domino's
The Move
Thin crust loaded with grilled chicken, two slices max — then add a boneless chicken order on the side to actually hit your protein. You came for pizza. Get the pizza. Just don't let it be the only protein on the table.
Thin Crust Chicken Pizza (2 slices)
Half the carbs of hand-tossed. Grilled chicken topping, marinara base. Two slices is a real portion. Resist the third.
~22g ~360 cal
Boneless Chicken — 8pc (Buffalo or Plain)
The protein filler. Buffalo or plain, not the sweet sauces. Closes the gap the pizza leaves. This is why you're having a good pizza night instead of a bad one.
~26g ~290 cal
The Combo: 2 Slices + 8pc Chicken
~48g protein for ~650 cal total. That's a legitimate pizza night without torching the week. Skip the crazy bread.
~48g ~650 cal
Shake Shack
The Move
Grilled Chicken Sandwich, no ChickShack sauce — swap it for mustard. That one swap saves ~100 cal and you won't miss it. The chicken itself is genuinely excellent. Going bunless on any burger is also always an option if you want to keep the carbs in check.
Grilled Chicken Sandwich
Full chicken breast, brioche bun, lettuce, pickles, cherry pepper sauce. No ChickShack sauce — mustard instead. One of the best fast food chicken sandwiches made, and the numbers hold up.
~40g ~390 cal
ShackBurger Single — No Bun
Beef patty, American, lettuce, tomato, ShackSauce in a lettuce wrap. High fat but manageable on the calories. Feels premium, because it is.
~22g ~340 cal
SmokeShack Single
Smoked bacon, cherry peppers, ShackSauce. Worth it occasionally — go bunless to keep it in check. This is the treat meal, not the everyday move.
~32g ~490 cal
Brewery & Tavern
The Move
Every pub menu has a grilled protein somewhere — find it and build around it. Order it with a house salad instead of fries. For drinks, go spirits with zero-sugar mixers or a light lager. The IPAs that are 250+ cal a pint add up fast. You're out to have a good time, not undo a week.
Burger — No Bun + Side Salad
Lettuce wrap if they offer it, otherwise eat it with a fork. House salad with balsamic on the side instead of fries. Easy swap, nobody at the table cares.
~35g ~480 cal
Blackened Chicken or Salmon
Best-case scenario on most pub menus. Ask for grilled or blackened, not fried. Swapping fries for a salad almost never costs extra — just ask.
~38g ~420 cal
Wings — 10–12pc (Naked or Buffalo)
Skip breaded. Naked or traditional buffalo only — the sweet glazes add 150+ cal with no payoff. Shareable, high-protein, easy to manage.
~40g ~480 cal
