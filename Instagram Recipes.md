## Keto High-Protein Cottage Cheese Flatbread
**By:** @courtney.ketorecipes · [View reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DSM1nZ4CUY4/)

**Ingredients**
- ¾ cup full-fat cottage cheese, well drained (200 g)
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp salt (not heaping)
- Italian herbs, to taste
- Olive oil, for greasing
- Filling (to taste): cream cheese, arugula, tomato slices, bacon, avocado, salt & pepper

**Instructions**
1. Place the cottage cheese in a fine mesh sieve or cheesecloth and let it drain for 10–15 minutes, gently pressing to remove excess liquid.
2. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C).
3. In a bowl, mix the drained cottage cheese with the eggs, salt, and Italian herbs until well combined.
4. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and lightly grease with olive oil.
5. Spread the mixture onto the baking sheet, shaping it into a thin rectangle.
6. Bake for 30 minutes, or until golden and set.
7. Let the flatbread cool slightly, add your desired fillings, and cut in half to serve.

**Notes:** Per flatbread (est.): 260 calories | 14g fat | 28g protein | 3g net carbs. Gluten-free and keto-friendly.

## High Protein Chicken Enchilada Bake
**By:** @shredhappens · [View reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DEfyOODSCwY/)

**Ingredients**
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 1 yellow onion, finely diced
- 1.5 heaping cups broccoli, finely diced
- 1 cup baby spinach, chopped
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 2 cups pre-cooked chicken
- 15 oz red enchilada sauce
- 3/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1 cup shredded Mexican cheese blend
- Optional toppings: avocado, cilantro, green onion, jalapeño

**Instructions**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F.

## 3-Minute Microwave Berry Crumble
**By:** @_emilysworld · [View reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C6g0LyUsVlB/)

**Ingredients**
- 125g frozen berries
- 1.5 tsp lemon juice
- 30g oats
- 25g vanilla protein powder
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tbsp syrup, honey or agave
- 15g peanut butter
- 1/2 tbsp almond milk (only if mix is too dry — add slowly)
- Greek yogurt, to serve

**Instructions**
1. Combine the frozen berries with the lemon juice and microwave for 1 minute.
2. Mix the oats, protein powder, cinnamon, syrup and peanut butter into a crumble topping.
3. Pour the crumble mixture over the warm berries and microwave for another 2 minutes. Top with Greek yogurt.

**Notes:** No microwave? Air fry 15 min at 150°C, or bake 15 min at 170°C fan. Substitutions: use oat flour instead of protein powder (add 1 tbsp syrup instead of 1/2 tbsp); swap peanut butter for melted butter or coconut oil.

## Peanut Butter Chocolate Discs
**By:** @thehomechefmomma · [View reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DBJzaCGSn5j/)

**Ingredients**
- 1 ripe banana, mashed
- 1 cup creamy peanut butter
- 1/8 cup honey
- 1/3 cup peanuts
- 1/2 cup plain whole-milk Greek yogurt
- 12 oz dark chocolate, melted
- Flaky sea salt (optional)

**Instructions**
1. In a medium bowl, mix the mashed banana, peanut butter, honey, peanuts and yogurt until fully combined.
2. Spoon the mix onto a parchment-lined baking sheet (makes 10–12) and flatten into discs.
3. Freeze for about an hour until fully hardened.
4. Coat each disc completely in melted chocolate, place back on parchment, sprinkle with sea salt if desired, and freeze a few minutes until set. Enjoy!

**Notes:** No-bake, high-protein sweet treat. Store in a freezer-safe container in the freezer. Estimated ~180 cal and 5g protein per disc.

## Tomato-Pesto Baked Feta Spaghetti Squash
**By:** @dishingouthealth · [View reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C6cDfyrujgA/)

**Ingredients**
- 1 medium spaghetti squash, halved lengthwise and seeded
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 8 oz cherry tomatoes (about 1 1/2 cups)
- 3 tbsp pesto
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- Pinch of crushed red pepper flakes
- 2 (2-oz) wedges of feta cheese
- Chopped fresh basil, to garnish

**Instructions**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Drizzle the inside of each squash boat with EVOO; season with salt and pepper.
2. In a bowl, combine the tomatoes, pesto, garlic, red pepper flakes, a drizzle of EVOO (1–2 tbsp) and a pinch of salt.
3. Place a feta wedge inside each squash half, then divide the tomato-pesto mixture between them.
4. Bake 50–55 minutes until the squash flesh is tender and the tomatoes burst.
5. Scrape the squash strands into the feta-tomato mixture with a fork; mix until combined. Garnish with fresh basil and extra cracked black pepper.

**Notes:** Pairs well with chicken meatballs or any protein. Vegetarian, low-carb and gluten-free.

## Tortilla Cheese-Crust Breakfast Pizza
**By:** @hubbybap · [View reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DVC2ne_iGbq/)

**Ingredients**
- 1 large flour tortilla
- 3 mozzarella string cheese sticks
- 1–2 tbsp pesto
- 1 egg
- Shredded mozzarella
- 3–4 cherry tomatoes, halved
- Egg wash and sesame seeds (optional)
- Fresh basil, to finish

**Instructions**
1. Lay the 3 string cheese sticks along the tortilla edges in a triangle. Fold the sides inward, rolling over the cheese to form a stuffed-crust triangle; secure corners with toothpicks.

**Notes:** Very flexible — swap the sauce or toppings to suit breakfast, snack or late-night mode.

**Ingredients**
- Chicken breast, cubed
- Olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, and spices, for the marinade
- Flatbread or pita
- Tzatziki sauce

## Jalapeño Popper Dip
**By:** @whatsmomcookin_ · [View reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/ClhTsq4Dlyr/)

**Ingredients**
- 1 block cream cheese, softened
- Sour cream
- 1 can diced jalapeños
- 2 fresh jalapeños, chopped
- 1 packet ranch dressing mix
- 1 lb cooked crumbled bacon
- 2 cups sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
- 1 cup mozzarella, shredded, plus more for topping

**Instructions**
1. Mix the cream cheese, sour cream, jalapeños, ranch mix, bacon, cheddar, and mozzarella in a large bowl.
2. Spread into a greased 9x11 baking dish and top with extra mozzarella.
3. Bake at 350°F for 30–35 minutes, until golden and bubbly. Serve with crackers.

## Creamy Spicy Garlic Noodles with Shrimp

- Shrimp, raw

- Coconut milk
- Instant ramen noodles
- Green onions, chopped

4. Top with green onions, parsley, and Parmesan.

## Chicken Gyro in a Loaf Pan

- Greek yogurt

- Olive oil
- White wine vinegar

- Oregano, salt, paprika, and pepper
- Pita bread
- Sliced tomatoes, red onions, and fresh parsley

## Ramen Lasagna
**By:** @fooddudescook · [View reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C0rYBuJLdG2/)

**Ingredients**
- Dry instant ramen noodle sheets (about 6)
- 1 jar (32 oz) marinara sauce
- Ricotta cheese
- Shredded mozzarella
- Pepperoni slices
- Italian seasoning
- 1/2 instant ramen seasoning packet (optional)

**Instructions**
1. Arrange dry ramen sheets in a glass baking dish to form a base layer.
2. Pour the marinara over the noodles and spread evenly. Dollop and smooth ricotta over the sauce, sprinkle generously with mozzarella, and layer on pepperoni. Dust with Italian seasoning (and half the ramen seasoning packet if using).
3. Bake about 50 minutes, until bubbly and golden. Let rest a few minutes before slicing.

## 2-Ingredient Parmesan Balls
**By:** @bobbyparrish · [View reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXsiV45kq9y/)

**Ingredients**
- 10 oz freshly grated Parmesan cheese
- 4 egg whites
- Marinara sauce, for serving

**Instructions**
1. Mix the Parmesan and egg whites until a dough forms.
2. Roll into 1-inch balls and freeze for 2 hours.
3. Air fry at 400°F for 8–10 minutes, until golden.
4. Serve immediately with marinara.

**Notes:** High protein and low carb. Viewers report they can be quite salty — use less cheese per bite or serve with a mild dip.

## Homemade Mozzarella (2 Ingredients)
**By:** @thedesignboy · [View reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C77D8LhoXYU/)

**Ingredients**
- 2 litres whole milk
- ~120 ml white vinegar

**Instructions**
1. Heat the milk to about 46°C.
2. Stir in the vinegar and let the curds form.
3. Drain the curds, then knead and shape into a ball.
4. Cool in ice water.

**Notes:** Some viewers note the result resembles paneer or ricotta more than true mozzarella (which uses rennet). The creator also makes a sandwich with the cheese: fresh tomato, basil, hot honey, and butter.

## High-Protein Air Fryer Bagels
**By:** @the.pediatric.dietitian · [View reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DIWZ19vu_Gn/)

**Ingredients** (per 1 bagel)
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
- 1/4 tsp baking powder

**Instructions**
1. Mix the flour, yogurt, and baking powder into a dough and knead.
2. Shape into a bagel ring and place in an air fryer basket lined with parchment.
3. Air fry at 350°F for 10 minutes, flip, and cook 5 more minutes.

**Notes:** About 147 calories and 9g protein per bagel. Also works in the oven at 350°F for 20 minutes.
